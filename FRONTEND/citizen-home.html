Preparedness Directives

  • 1. Emergency Go-Bag Keep a bag packed with first aid, a flashlight, batteries, non-perishable food, and essential documents.
  • 2. Know Your Sector Familiarize yourself with the evacuation routes and designated safe zones in your municipality.
  • 3. Accurate Reporting When reporting an incident, ensure precise GPS latitude/longitude is captured for rapid response targeting.
  • 4. Trust Official Sources Rely purely on this portal or verified government broadcasts. Avoid spreading unverified information on social platforms.
